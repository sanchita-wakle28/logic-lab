What is a proposition?

A proposition is a declarative sentence that is either true or false — never both, and never neither. "It is raining" is a proposition. "Please close the door" and "Is it raining?" are not, because they carry no truth value. We build compound propositions out of simple ones using logical connectives:

¬p NOT p
p ∧ q p AND q
p ∨ q p OR q
p → q p IMPLIES q
p ↔ q p IFF q
